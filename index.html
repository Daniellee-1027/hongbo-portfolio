02 / The person behind the projects

Curious about technology.
Grounded in people.

My path started in automation and electrical engineering at Zhejiang University. Building an early venture in mobile-device security taught me to ask a question that still shapes my work: what does this technology actually help someone do?

That question led me to research in household energy management, then to venture capital, where I explored how energy technologies find their way into real markets. At UC Berkeley, Development Engineering brings those interests together: rigorous technical work, an understanding of context, and attention to who benefits.

I'm especially interested in the space between an energy model and everyday life: the household behind the load curve, the health facility behind the demand estimate, and the decisions that connect them.
